**Nothing technical.** Just this:

> The landing page has a **"Business" card** that goes nowhere right now. The client wants a **/business page** that speaks to a **manager or boss** — someone who would say *"Hey team, everyone forward your spam to this address"* — instead of speaking to an individual person.

That's it. It's purely a **marketing page** with different wording aimed at a different audience.
